Hostel Rules & Resident Guidelines

Simple policies to keep our community peaceful, clean, and safe for everyone.

Timings & Visitor Policy

  • Main biometric gate closes at 10:30 PM (late entry allowed with prior warden intimation).
  • Visitors and parents are permitted only in the ground-floor lobby between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
  • Overnight stay of non-residents inside rooms is strictly prohibited.

Conduct, Safety & Notice Period

  • Maintain quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM for fellow students and professionals.
  • Smoking, alcohol, and illegal substances are strictly banned on the premises.
  • A 30-day prior notice is required for vacating and full security deposit refund.
